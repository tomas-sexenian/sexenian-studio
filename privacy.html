Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026

Short version: this site has no backend and no database. It's a static page. We don't collect account data, we don't run analytics or ad trackers, and the "Get in touch" button just opens your own email app — nothing passes through our servers.

1. Controller

Sexenian Studio, operated by Tomás Sexenian, an individual based in Solymar, Ciudad de la Costa, Canelones, Uruguay. Contact: cuentastomassexenian@gmail.com.

2. What this site does and doesn't collect

  • Theme preference: your dark/light choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device and we never see it.
  • Contact: the "Get in touch" link opens a pre-filled email in your own email client (mailto:). Whatever you send goes directly to our inbox through your email provider — this website has no contact form, no server, and never stores or transmits that message itself.
  • No accounts, no cookies for tracking, no analytics, no advertising pixels. We don't know who visits this site or how many times.

3. Hosting and fonts

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log standard technical access data (IP address, browser type, request timestamps) as part of normal web server operation — this is GitHub's infrastructure, not something we configure or access; see GitHub's own privacy statement. This page's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com), which means your browser makes a direct request to Google's servers to fetch them, exposing your IP address to Google in the same way any embedded third-party resource would.

4. Links to other sites

This site links to Remy, Mercado Intel, and Sibyl — each a separate product with its own Privacy Policy governing what happens if you actually use that product — and to external profiles (LinkedIn, GitHub). Their privacy practices are their own; this policy covers only this portfolio site.

5. Your rights

Since this site doesn't collect or store personal data beyond what's described above, there's little to request access to or delete. If you have any privacy question or concern anyway, email cuentastomassexenian@gmail.com and we'll respond directly.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
